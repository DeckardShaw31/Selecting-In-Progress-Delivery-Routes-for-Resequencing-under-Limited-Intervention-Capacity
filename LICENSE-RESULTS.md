This covers the route- and cell-level results, the reports, and the pre-specification documents. The non-commercial term follows from the licences of the source data:

| Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| 2021 Amazon Last Mile Routing Research Challenge (Merchán et al., 2024) | CC BY-NC 4.0 | route structures of all simulations |
| LaDe (Wu et al., 2024), pickup part | CC BY-NC 4.0, as stated in the dataset paper | Gate 4 |
| Planned and driven routes (Konovalenko, Hvattum and Iversen, 2026) | CC BY 4.0 | Gate 2d and the calibration of the route-wide share |

When you reuse these results, cite the paper (see `CITATION.cff`) and the data sources above.

The raw data are not redistributed here; `data/README.md` explains where to obtain them.
